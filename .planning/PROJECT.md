## Problem Statement

Indian Gen Z (18-28) is drowning in information but starving for context.
- Inshorts: speed but zero context
- Google News: comprehensive but overwhelming + filter bubble
- The Ken: depth but ₹999+/month and not Gen Z-toned
- Social media: viral but unverified

## Solution

Gen Z Pulse = Human-curated x AI-assisted context engine:
1. What happened (60-word summary)
2. Why it matters (100-word context layer)
3. What comes next (Consequence Engine)
4. What you can do (Action Pathways)
5. How the story evolves (Story Arc)
