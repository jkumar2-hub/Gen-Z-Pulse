# Gen Z Pulse — Phase 5 Plan (Community & Content)

## Objective
Transform Gen Z Pulse from a passive consumption platform into an active campus ecosystem. By launching the Student Journalist Network and the College Leaderboard, we empower users to create content and compete for campus pride, driving virality and retention.

## 1. Student Journalist Network (Content Creation)
- **Task 1.1:** Create a `/contribute` page where students can pitch news stories, campus events, or opinion pieces.
- **Task 1.2:** Implement a modern, Gen-Z focused submission form asking for:
  - Headline
  - Why this matters (Context)
  - Category (Campus, Tech, etc.)
  - Source links
- **Task 1.3:** Build a success state highlighting that their piece will be reviewed by the editorial board, and if published, they earn the "Campus Editor" badge.

## 2. College Leaderboard (News IQ Gamification)
- **Task 2.1:** Enhance the existing `/iq` (News IQ) page or create a `/leaderboard` tab.
- **Task 2.2:** Build a dynamic `Leaderboard` UI displaying top colleges based on cumulative News IQ scores.
- **Task 2.3:** Include rank tracking (e.g., "GITAM University is #1", "SRM is #2") with slick Framer Motion row-entrance animations.
- **Task 2.4:** Add a "Verify College Email" CTA block on the leaderboard to incentivize students to link their university email address to contribute to their college's rank.

## 3. State & Roadmap Updates
- **Task 3.1:** Mark Phase 5 tasks as complete in `ROADMAP.md` and `STATE.md`.

---
**Verification Requirements:**
- A student can successfully fill out and submit a story pitch via `/contribute`.
- The `/iq` page visually ranks colleges in a stunning glassmorphism leaderboard.
