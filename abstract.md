# Gen Z Pulse — Executive Summary (Demo Version)

**Gen Z Pulse** is India's Context Engine for Gen Z. It transforms traditional, overwhelming news into an intuitive, swipe-based interface designed for the attention economy, without sacrificing depth or credibility.

## 🚀 Core Differentiators
1. **The 60-Word Pulse:** Every story is summarized into exactly 60 words for immediate consumption.
2. **Context Engine (Deep Dive):** For users who want more, a single tap expands the story with:
   - **Why it matters** (Macro impact)
   - **What's next** (Forward-looking predictions)
   - **What you can do** (Actionable insights)
3. **Source DNA:** A built-in credibility and bias scanner that proves the reliability of every story, countering the era of fake news.
4. **Story Arcs:** News is rarely a single event. Story Arcs connect ongoing events (e.g., "The AI Boom" or "Climate Policy") into chronologically linked timelines.
5. **News IQ™ Gamification:** A weekly quiz that tests reading comprehension, tied to a collegiate leaderboard to drive viral campus adoption.

## 💼 Business Model
- **B2C Subscription (Pulse PRO):** Premium tier offering unlimited deep dives, exclusive Story Arcs, and ad-free listening.
- **B2B Analytics (Pulse Brands):** Aggregated, anonymized sentiment and trend data sold to major consumer brands wanting to understand Gen Z behavior in real-time.

## 🛠️ Demo Capabilities
- **Swipe Feed (`/feed`):** Infinite, Tinder-style card swiping for news consumption.
- **Dynamic Theming:** Dark mode (default) and Light mode (toggled via Profile).
- **Audio TTS:** Playback controls to listen to news on the go.
- **Gamified Profile (`/profile`):** Read streaks, saved stories, and News IQ ranks.
