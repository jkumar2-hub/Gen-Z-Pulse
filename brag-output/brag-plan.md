# Brag Plan: Gen Z Pulse

## What is this app?
Gen Z Pulse is India's first real-time context engine for the TikTok generation — turning complex breaking news into a kinetic 3-layer swipe stream with zero clickbait, instant AI-generated multi-angle summaries, Source DNA™ credibility ratings, and verified publisher citations.

## The angle
"Context Over Clutter." While traditional media alienates young readers with dense academic jargon, aggressive paywalls, and partisan echo chambers, Gen Z Pulse rebuilds news from first principles: 15-second visual punch cards, Pinterest-style topic onboarding, interactive audio synthesizers, and verified links that never 404.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
An explosive zoom into the animated brutalist landing hero:  
`"Context Over Clutter. News Built for the TikTok Generation."`  
Neon pink and cyan chromatics with the live production badge glowing at `gen-z-pulse-kappa.vercel.app`.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Pinterest-Style Topic Onboarding**: Glowing category tiles for Finance, AI, Campus & Careers, Climate, and Culture with instant feed adaptation.
- **Kinetic TikTok Swipe Feed (`/feed`)**: Framer Motion vertical gestures, category chromatic badges, verified publisher link chips, and instantaneous card-level `✨ AI Summary`.
- **3-Layer Depth Dial (`/story/[id]`)**: Interactive tactile switcher between 15s Pulse (60 words), 60s Context (macroeconomic consequences), and 3m Deep Dive (structural market analysis).
- **Multi-Angle AI Synthesis Engine**: 3-point quantitative TL;DR, conversational No-Cap Take, and high-stakes Career Impact analysis with 1-click clipboard copy.
- **Source DNA™ & Bias Meter**: Objective 10-point credibility score and 4-way political lean radar to eliminate misinformation.
- **Fail-Safe Verified Citations**: Direct links to primary publisher reporting (Economic Times, Reuters, The Hindu, Apple, Space.com) paired with a live Google News syndication hub.
- **Gamified News IQ™ & Campus Leaderboard (`/iq`)**: Interactive comprehension challenges ranking colleges across India.

## Outro / punchline
"Gen Z doesn't hate news. We hate bad signal-to-noise. Try India's fastest context engine live at gen-z-pulse-kappa.vercel.app."

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry**: Rapid 5-second Pinterest onboarding selecting topics.
2. **Key Action**: Vertical swipe on feed, opening the tactile Depth Dial, and triggering the instant AI Summary engine.
3. **Result**: Audio synthesis playback, fact-checked Source DNA verification, and direct verified external article navigation.

- Restraint rule: Music never competes with speech frequencies (ducked between 500Hz - 4kHz).
